# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- `Cubes.pivotOver` in `FSharp.Wasm.Live`: the pivot of facts that are written into in place (a line of the ledger is corrected). The owner of the facts writes the number into the array and a `revision` (a function that reads a `Source<int>`, or a calc of the graph that applies the correction) says so by another number; the cube is then built again from the facts (one pass), and not before. `Cubes.pivot` is `pivotOver` with a revision that nobody raises. A test under .NET and as WebAssembly GC (174).
- `FSharp.Wasm.Telemetry`: the readings of instruments for dashboards and SCADA. `Sample` (time, value, quality `Good`/`Uncertain`/`Bad`), `Limits` (low-low, low, high, high-high, a deadband and a delay), `Alarm.level` (a limit is entered at the limit and left when the value is back by the deadband), `Alarm.step` (the life of an alarm by ISA-18.2: raised, acknowledged, returned, cleared, escalated and eased, a delay that starts again after a dip, a bad reading as a fault at once, shelving), `Alarm.acknowledge` and `Alarm.run`, and `Historian.deadband` (a history kept small: the readings that moved, the changes of quality, a heartbeat). Tests under .NET and as WasmGC (docs/telemetry.md).
- `FSharp.Wasm.Live`: filter, group by (count, sum, min, max, mean), the pivot of facts, a plan written over a measure of a cube, and the visible lines of a hierarchy, as calcs of the graph engine; a table that only grew costs its new rows. `Source.Version` in `FSharp.Wasm.Graph`. 8 tests under .NET and as WasmGC.
- `FSharp.Wasm.Formula`: the cells are calcs of the graph engine (`Sheet`, over text that can live in a `Doc`); `Book` keeps its API. A formula depends on what it read, so the branch `IF` does not take is no dependency.
- `FSharp.Wasm.Graph`: an incremental data graph. `Engine` with `Source<'a>` and `Calc<'a>` (a calc reads what it needs and is run again, once each and after the cells it reads, only when something it read came out different; reads may differ from run to run; cells that read each other have the value of a cycle; a chain a hundred thousand long is a walk, not a call stack; `Flushed` says how many were reached, ran and changed). `Doc`: nodes with registers, maps, lists (a sequence CRDT) and columns, changed by `Patch`es of `Op`s that are data (stamped, last writer wins, the same graph in any order) whose inverse is made when they are applied; `Timeline` keeps the tape, undo and redo. Runs under .NET and as WasmGC (151 tests each).
- `Cube.leaf` and `Cube.withLeaves` in `FSharp.Wasm.Frame`: one leaf cell of a measure read back from the prefix sums, and a cube whose leaf cells are replaced by values of their own (a plan that overrides a budget): the difference of a leaf is added to the prefix sums below and to the right of it, so the totals of every group that holds it move by exactly that and no others do; the measure's sums are copied, the other measures' shared. 2 tests (130 in all).
- `FSharp.Wasm.Formula`: the formulas of a spreadsheet. `Parser.parse` (references `A1` and `$A$1`, ranges, names, numbers with exponents and percent, text with doubled quotes, calls, the operators with their precedence, errors with the position of the first problem), `Parser.print`, `Parser.shift` (a copied formula), `Eval.eval` over an `Env` (cells, names and functions of the host) with the spreadsheet's errors as values, 18 functions (SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, PRODUCT, ABS, INT, SQRT, ROUND, MOD, POWER, IF, IFERROR, AND, OR, NOT, CONCAT), and `Book`: cells that hold a number, text or a formula; `Set` recalculates the cell and what reads it, once each and after the cells it reads, and returns the cells whose value changed with what they were (the patch of the edit); cycles are `#CYCLE!` for every cell of the cycle; a chain as long as the sheet (16 384 rows) needs no deep stack. 39 tests under .NET and as WasmGC (docs/formula.md).
- `FSharp.Wasm.Actors`: a supervisor (`OneForOne`, `OneForAll`, restart intensity, give up) and a pool of workers (a mailbox per worker, at-least-once delivery with an idempotent end, the jobs of a dead worker back to the queue) as pure state machines: an event and a state give a new state and commands. 13 tests under .NET and as WasmGC, among them 120 seeded runs each with workers that crash at random and answers that arrive late and out of order; docs/actors.md. Uses lists where `Map` belongs, until fsharp-wasm#33 (Map as a record field) is fixed.
- `FSharp.Wasm.Design`: the look of the platform as data. Seven OKLCH colour ramps, the roles (`Tokens`: page, surface, raised, line, control, text, heading, muted, primary and its states, link, accent, secondary, signal, focus, success, warning, danger) for a light and a dark theme, the scales (`Scale`), the contrast every pair of roles has to keep (`Tokens.checks`, 29 pairs in each theme, all above their minimum on 2026-11-24), and `Export.css` / `Export.json`; `tools/design` writes `design/tokens.css` and `design/tokens.json` (`--check` fails when they are stale, as in CI); 14 tests under .NET and as WasmGC; docs/design.md.
- `Color.oklchInGamut` and `Color.fitOklch` in `FSharp.Wasm.Ui`: a colour sRGB cannot show gives up chroma, and keeps lightness and hue, instead of having a channel clipped (which turns the hue); 1 test.
- `FSharp.Wasm.Frame`: hierarchies of ordered leaves, a cube of prefix sums over two of them (`Cube.value`: the total of a row group over a column group, by sum, average, first or last), `Facts.pivot` and `Facts.entries` (drill-down), formulas over measures (`Expr`; a division by zero is `nan`), and `Format.amount`; 8 tests under .NET and as WasmGC, among them every pair of nodes against a plain sum of the leaves.
- `FSharp.Wasm.Icons`: icons as typed shapes (path, circle, ellipse, rect, line, polyline, polygon) on a 24 x 24 grid, `Icons.svg` and `Icons.element` (the tag and attributes of a shape, for a DOM view or a canvas), and 51 icons of Lucide 1.77.0 (ISC; those it took from Feather are MIT) generated by `tools/icons/generate.mjs`; licence text in `LICENSES/ISC-MIT-lucide.txt`; 4 tests under .NET and as WasmGC; docs/icons.md.
- `FSharp.Wasm.Edit`: the edits of a document as data (operations with inverses, `Ops.drop`, `Ops.insertAt` for a new item put at a place, `History`, `Inline`), 16 tests under .NET and as WasmGC; docs/edit.md.
- docs/consolidation.md: how a copy is merged without breaking anyone and without losing anything.
- The F# base library of fsharp-wasm as its own repository, moved from `fable-library-wasmgc/` of the compiler repository:
  `FSharp.Wasm.Core` (Interop, Option, Result, Map, Set, System.Collections.Generic, ListHelpers, MathHelpers) with one compile
  list (`FSharp.Wasm.Core.props`) that replaces the two the compiler repository kept (`Fable.LibraryWasmGc.fsproj`,
  `Collections.props`).
- Apache License 2.0.

### Changed
- Merged with `develop`, which already carried a library of the same purpose: the System.* surface in F# (`Interfaces`, `Exception`,
  `Math`, `Task`, `RuntimeHelper`) and its Expecto tests are part of `FSharp.Wasm.Core` now, as the group `Bcl`. Where both
  libraries defined the same types (`Dictionary`, `Stack`, `StringBuilder`, a placeholder `ResizeArray`) the one the compiler builds
  and tests is kept — `Collections/System.Collections.Generic.fs` — and `StringBuilder` is the compiler's own (`src/Bcl` of
  fsharp-wasm). `Stack` gained `TryPop`, `TryPeek` and a constructor from a sequence, which the old tests asked for.
- The project is `FSharp.Wasm.Core.fsproj` (it was `FSharp.WasmCore.fsproj` with the assembly name `FSharp.Wasm.Core`).
- The nested submodules `vendor/fsharp` and `vendor/fsharp-wasm` are gone: nothing here used them, and `vendor/fsharp` pinned a commit that
  does not exist in the organization, so a recursive checkout of any repository that contains this one failed.

### Removed
- Duplicates and leftovers of the old directory: a second `Interop.fs` (the `Core/` one is the superset), an int-only `Map.fs`
  next to the generic `Collections/Map.fs`, an unused `Seq.fs` (the backend handles `Seq` itself), the empty `WasmGcModule.fs`
  and a shell script that had been committed as `current_interop_location.txt`.
